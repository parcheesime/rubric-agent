## Tavily Evaluation (July 2026)

Objective:
Evaluate Tavily as a discovery mechanism for educational rubrics and syllabi.

Outcome:
Rejected.

Findings:
- Search results heavily favored articles about writing rubrics and syllabi rather than the documents themselves.
- Discovery quality was inconsistent across institutions.
- Structured repositories (IIIF, institutional archives, OCW, OER APIs) provide higher-quality sources and better provenance.

Decision:
Future collectors will prioritize structured repositories and APIs over general web search.
